## A. Ranked edge hypotheses (mechanism-first)

### H1. Scanner-lag rental window (test first — zero new data needed)
Alert infrastructure + human/funding latency forces 5–30 min of inflow AFTER a
visibility event (first top-N entry, HOD print, halt reopen). Predictable object:
drift in minutes-since-promotion buckets (0–10/10–30/30–60), NOT rank level.
Why now: hindsight-leader minutes (+30/+51bps) are this seen from the future; rank-1
chase tested level (mixed) never recency. Falsifier: mb100 flat across event-age
buckets month-blocked. Test: bucket existing 165-name panel + Friday names by
promotion age; metric = mb100/bucket + FP. Cost: none — event timestamps exist.

### H2. Participation management / narrow-base rental (test first, parallel-safe)
No morning pick (selection ≈ 0 pooled). Enter top-5 @10ET mechanically, tiny size;
all intelligence in stay-rules (time-stop, trailing giveback cap, −100bps abort,
15:30 flat). Target: captured fraction of mb100 net of FP giveback. Falsifier (frozen
single rule): mean capture ≤ +150bps or median ≤ 0 or FP/captured > 1.5 pooled.
Why now: MFE positive all 9 blocks while terminal flips = directly a stay-problem
fact. Data already logged. Second leg: narrow-base filter (PM narrow tercile 0.71
extension; entry-bar range; near-VWAP) ranking excursion-per-unit-heat.

### H3. Turnover-clock exhaustion / overhang age (most inevitable, needs float work)
Fragility = f(distance-above-VWAP, 1/turnover-since-emergence, promotion age).
Low-turnover extended = fast MFE then decay; high-turnover near-VWAP = sustain.
Falsifier: mb200-capped shows no monotone hazard ordering by turnover tercile,
month-blocked. Blocker: no PIT float — use cum-$vol-since-promotion ÷ ADV baseline
(build_features def) as the clock; yfinance float last resort with sensitivity-off test.

### H4. Exhaustion avoidance as primary skill (veto, not pick)
Predict P(unharvestable: mb100<200 or immediate −100 touch <15min). Frozen veto:
wide-PM tercile AND (halt-thin <8 bars by 10ET OR warrant/penny flag).
Falsifier: vetoed mb100 within 100bps of kept, or recall loss >25% of top-quartile
mb100 names. Why now: FP ≥1.2/name is the binding cost; MFE near-universal (P≈1.00)
makes refusal-precision the scarce resource. E1 trigger-gap (0.45 vs 0.28) hints
selection acts on setup frequency — veto the tapes where nothing fires.

### H5. Halt-queue resolution (magnitude edge, exchange mechanics)
Reopen MUST print volatility; sign follows pre-halt velocity × reopen-bar imbalance.
Plus structural FP cut: bar-less/warrant/penny names (16% of Friday promotions)
vetoed as untradable, not modeled. Falsifier: post-reopen |excursion| ≤ matched
non-halt promotion windows. SIP-only feasible (bar-absence runs + reopen shape).

### H6. Failed-move fade (conditions on post-rip info — never competes with selection)
After MFE ≥ +800bps + stall (3 compressed 5-min bars or halt-resume failure), fade to
15:30ET, +100bps adverse stop. Monetizes Friday −1025 terminal + tail-~0 directly.
Falsifier: mean fade ≤ 0 pooled, trigger < 0.5/day, stop-rate > 60%.

### H7. Transition prediction (handoff as unit) / repeated harvesting (legs portfolio)
H7a: join outside-top-10→top-5 crossers (30-min hold), cut top-3→outside-top-10
droppers; 42% of leaders emerge late so handoffs are the real unit. Needs event
logging (small wiring). H7b: 3–5 legs on one committed name/day; legs-sum vs
buy-hold. Most faithful to retail, most complex — last in queue.

## 2026-09-07 — runner phenomenology + first positive gate (data-driven)

**Phenomenology (141 runners ≥60% open→close, 2025-05..12 + 2026-03, 172 days):**
- Stable frequency: 1.2–1.5 such runners/day, every month, no dead months.
- Move takes ALL DAY: med open→high 322min; half the move done only by 11:25; only 15%
  half-done by 10:00. Afternoon share of move: median 55%.
- Halts are the fingerprint: 84% of runners halt; dose-response (0 halts → +83% mean,
  11+ halts → +177%). Retrace ≥30% mid-move didn't kill 24/141 (mean +244% finish).
- Clock conclusion: 10:00-anchored snapshot designs structurally truncated the object.

**First positive gate (pre-registered next-bar-open from 10:30):**
- Signature = gain≥15% by 10:30 AND ≥1 halt-gap by 10:30 (SIP bar-hole proxy). n=281.
- Rest-of-day fwd mean +2.8% (10× CTRL +0.3%), MFE med +11%, MAE med −8%.
- Month-blocked: 7/9 positive (+243..+2127bps; worst −372). Win 48% — payoff is in the tail.
- 50/281 had MFE≥30% after 10:30 (mean finish +32%). Tail-harvest structure: median ~0,
  mean driven by MFE-tail — extraction requires stay-rules (H2 discipline) on top of the gate.
