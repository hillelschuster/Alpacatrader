# H001–H005 Failure Synthesis — 2025-07-02 to 2025-07-31 (14d train / 7d test, 20bps rt)

Five continuation hypotheses — pullback reclaim (H001), HOD breakout (H002), gain×volume×TOD (H003), micro-pullback above VWAP (H004), rank persistence (H005) — all killed after realistic costs.

**Universal:** Net expectancy negative at 1–30m for every signal. Only 60m showed thin gross +0.12% (H004) to +0.42% (H001) that vanished net or flipped OOS. Win rates 38–48% after costs; mean reversion dominates top-gainer drift. Signal counts large (H001 1,272; H002 41,947; H003 38,522; H004 20,800) except H005 persistence (n=278 vs brief n=284) but still both net negative — persistence merely less bad (-0.87% vs -4.46% h60).

**Mean reversion + extremes fail:** The most extended cohort (20%+) was worst: H001 h60 -3.39% net, H002 h60 -0.80%, H003 high-vol open -7.64% (n=58). Parabolic = fade, not continuation.

**Cost drag:** Gross edges 0.00–0.20% erased by 20bps. Any executable edge needs >20bps gross — none achieved.

**OOS decay universal:** All flipped train>test: H001 h60 +0.66%→-0.14%, H002 +0.08%→-0.20%, H004 -0.06%→-0.15%. Train/test top-5 overlap 1/5 (H003 at 15m and 60m). Pockets are noise.

**Inverted volume:** H003 high dollar volume underperformed low at all horizons (h60 high 0.06% vs low 1.46% diff -1.41%). High attention = distribution, not conviction.

**TOD misfire:** 09:30–10:00 worst, not best (H003 -0.06% h60 vs 10–12 best +1.28%). Mid-morning pockets (H001 10–11 h60 +1.72% net) not persistent. VWAP (H004 h60 net -0.08%, depth split identical) and rank persistence filters added no edge.

**Lesson:** Next hypotheses must invert (fade extremes), use TOD-normalized RVOL not naïve terciles, test contraction precursors not reactive extremes, and exploit failed moves, not clean breaks.
